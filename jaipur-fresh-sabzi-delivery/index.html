How to Keep Fresh Sabzi Fresh at Home

Proper storage is important after vegetables arrive at your home. Different vegetables have different storage requirements.

Store Vegetables According to Their Type

Some vegetables are better suited to refrigeration, while others can be stored in a cool and dry place. Follow suitable storage practices for each vegetable.

Avoid Excess Moisture

Excess moisture can affect some vegetables during storage. Where appropriate, keep produce dry and properly ventilated.

Use Leafy Vegetables Earlier

Leafy vegetables and other delicate produce may need to be used sooner than vegetables that naturally have a longer storage life.

Buy According to Your Needs

Purchasing quantities according to your actual consumption can help reduce unnecessary food waste.
