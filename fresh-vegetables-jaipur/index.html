How to Choose Fresh Vegetables

Whether you purchase vegetables online or from a traditional market, knowing what to look for can help you make better grocery choices.

Check the Appearance

Look for vegetables with a natural appearance suitable for their variety. Avoid produce showing obvious spoilage, excessive damage or unusual discoloration.

Consider Firmness

Many vegetables should have an appropriate level of firmness for their type. Excessive softness can sometimes indicate that the vegetable may not be at its best.

Look for Visible Damage

Cuts, bruises, excessive moisture and visible spoilage can affect vegetable quality and storage life.

Choose According to Your Needs

Buy vegetables according to how much your household normally consumes. Purchasing reasonable quantities can help reduce unnecessary food waste.

Store Vegetables Properly

Different vegetables require different storage conditions. After receiving your vegetables, store them according to their type and use them within a suitable period.

How to Keep Vegetables Fresh at Home

Buying fresh vegetables is only one part of maintaining their quality. Proper storage after purchase can also make a difference.

Store Vegetables According to Their Type

Different vegetables have different storage requirements. Some vegetables may need refrigeration, while others can be kept in a cool and dry place.

Avoid Excess Moisture

Excess moisture can affect some vegetables during storage. Where appropriate, keep vegetables dry and provide suitable ventilation.

Use Delicate Vegetables Earlier

Leafy vegetables and other delicate produce may need to be used sooner than vegetables that naturally have a longer storage life.

Avoid Buying More Than You Need

Buying according to your actual household consumption can help reduce waste and make it easier to use vegetables while they are in good condition.
